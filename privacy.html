Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Santex AI ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you use our website or services, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information We Collect

  • Contact details you provide directly (name, email, phone number, business name) when you request a consultation or sign up for a plan.
  • Usage data collected automatically through analytics tools (pages visited, time on site, device/browser type).
  • Campaign and account data connected through the platforms we manage on your behalf (e.g. Google Ads, Meta Ads, Google Business Profile), limited to what's needed to deliver our services.

How We Use Your Information

  • To deliver, manage, and improve the marketing services you've signed up for.
  • To communicate with you about your account, campaigns, and billing.
  • To analyze site performance and improve our own website and offering.

Data Sharing

We don't sell your personal data. We share information only with the third-party platforms required to run your campaigns (e.g. Google, Meta) and service providers who help us operate (hosting, analytics, payment processing), each bound by their own confidentiality and data protection obligations.

Data Retention & Security

We retain your data for as long as your account is active, or as needed to comply with legal obligations. We use industry-standard measures (SSL encryption, access controls) to protect your data, but no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

Your Rights

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting us. Depending on your location, you may have additional rights under applicable data protection law (e.g. UAE PDPL, GDPR).

Contact Us

Questions about this policy? Reach out via our Get Started page and we'll get back to you.

Data Security

Last updated: August 2026

Protecting your business data is core to how we operate. Here's an overview of the measures we take.

How We Protect Your Data

All data in transit is encrypted via SSL/TLS — including this website, which runs fully over HTTPS.

Access to client accounts (Google Ads, Meta Business, Google Business Profile) is granted only through official platform permissions — we never ask for or store your account passwords directly.

Internal access to client data is limited to team members directly working on your account.

Our infrastructure runs on reputable hosting with regular security scanning and malware protection.

Payment Security

We don't store your card details directly. Payments are processed through PCI-compliant third-party payment providers.

Incident Response

In the unlikely event of a data breach affecting your information, we'll notify you promptly and take immediate steps to contain and remediate the issue, in line with applicable law.

Your Role

We recommend using strong, unique passwords and enabling two-factor authentication on any platform accounts (Google, Meta) you connect with us.

  This page describes our current practices in general terms. As you formalize security certifications (e.g. ISO 27001) or specific vendor details, update this page to reflect them accurately.

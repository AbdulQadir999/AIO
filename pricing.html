FAQ

Questions?
We've Got Answers.

How quickly will I see results?
Most clients see measurable improvements in leads and traffic within the first 30 days. SEO results compound over time, while paid ads typically show improvements within the first 1–2 weeks of optimization.
Do I need to sign a contract?
No contracts, ever. We earn your business every month. You can cancel anytime with 30 days notice, and we'll wrap up all your campaigns cleanly.
What does the setup process look like?
Onboarding takes about 48 hours. We connect your accounts, run an AI audit, and have your first campaigns live within 5 business days. Our team handles everything — you don't need to be technical.
Can I upgrade or downgrade my plan?
Absolutely. You can change your plan at any time. Upgrades take effect immediately, and downgrades apply at the start of your next billing cycle.
Is ad spend included in the pricing?
No — ad spend is separate and goes directly to Google and Meta. Our fee covers management, optimization, and strategy. We typically recommend a minimum ad spend of AED 1,800/month to see meaningful results.
What industries do you work with?
We work with businesses across most industries — local services, e-commerce, professional services, healthcare, real estate, and more. If you're not sure if we're a fit, book a free audit and we'll tell you honestly.
